A craftsman's hand on your fibreglass boat

Fibreglass (GRP) boats are durable but face osmosis, gelcoat fading and cracks. We provide full service in exactly the areas where we're strongest.

Services for your fibreglass boat

  • Fibreglass repair & osmosis treatment
  • Painting & antifouling
  • Teak / non-slip deck
  • Interior refit
  • Winterising & maintenance

Common jobs

Osmosis treatment, crack/break repair, gelcoat renewal and underwater structural repair are our specialty on fibreglass boats. For resin compatibility, see polyester vs epoxy.
