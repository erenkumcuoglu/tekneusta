Boat Cleaning and Detailing

Before salt and UV take their toll — professional interior-exterior cleaning, compound-polish and protective care keep your boat's look and value.

Get a Free Quote

Cleaning is the first step in protection

Detailing isn't just cosmetic; it's the cheapest care that protects a boat's surface, value and life. At Tekne Usta we treat cleaning as part of holistic care — we don't just polish the surface, we protect it.

What we do

  • Exterior: removing salt, dirt and stains; restoring gloss with compound-polish.
  • Gelcoat protection: a protective wax after cleaning shields against UV and oxidation.
  • Interior: cleaning the cabin, upholstery and surfaces; mould and damp measures.
  • Detail: stainless, glass and fixture care.

In hands that know surfaces

Compound-polish and protection must be done with the right technique and products to protect the gelcoat; an aggressive polish thins it. We handle the surface with our fibreglass repair experience, without harm.

Why regular detailing?

Salt and UV, if neglected, permanently wear the gelcoat and upholstery. Regular detailing postpones a full gelcoat renewal or re-upholstery for years — the cheapest form of protection. For detail, see our detailing article.
