Boat Painting & Antifouling

From below-waterline antifouling to superyacht-grade topside paint — with the right primer system, clean masking and a flawless finish.

Get a Free Quote

Paint is both the boat's shield and its identity

A good paint job doesn't just look good; it protects the boat from UV, salt and marine growth. At Tekne Usta we treat surface preparation as seriously as the paint itself — because a lasting finish starts with correct sanding, priming and masking.

Our painting services

  • Antifouling — choosing and applying the right antifouling below the waterline
  • Full topside painting — multi-coat systems for hull and superstructure
  • Gelcoat & epoxy primer — building the surface on a sound base
  • Boot stripe, name and graphics — crisp lines with clean masking

Choosing the right antifouling

Not every antifouling suits every boat. Together we decide whether you need a hard matrix or a self-polishing type based on your boat's material, speed and the water it sits in. See our antifouling guide for detail.

Price and timing

Painting cost depends on boat length, number of coats and the extent of surface prep. An antifouling refresh takes a few days; a full topside repaint 1–3 weeks. We give a firm quote after inspecting the surface.

Antifouling types: which for which boat?

The right antifouling depends on how the boat is used. The table below sums up the main types (for detail, see the antifouling selection guide).

TypeSuited toTrait
Hard matrixFast / often hauled / racingNon-eroding, needs occasional sanding
Self-polishingCruising / mid speedErodes in a controlled way, no build-up
AblativeLightly usedSoft, economical
Copper-free (aluminium)Aluminium hullsPrevents corrosion

The secret to lasting paint: surface prep

Most of the cost and quality is in the prep, not the paint. Without correct sanding, degreasing, filling and priming, even the most expensive paint soon blisters. We quote itemised — prep, primer, coat count and labour shown separately — so you avoid cheap-looking jobs that end up half-done (see painting cost).

Topside: gelcoat or paint?

When renewing a faded surface, gelcoat and paint serve different aims. If the surface is sound, gelcoat renewal is economical; for a colour change or superyacht gloss, 2K polyurethane paint is preferred. To decide, see our gelcoat or paint article.
