The deck is a boat's first impression

A teak deck is the most eye-catching surface on a boat, for both looks and safety. Over the years teak thins, seams open and water starts to seep below. At Tekne Usta we lay new teak, renew old decks and repair seams with meticulous craftsmanship.

Our teak services

  • New teak decking — natural teak or maintenance-free synthetic teak options
  • Old teak renewal — sanding, seam renewal and surface restoration
  • Seam (caulking) renewal — removing and remaking leaking black seams
  • Regular teak care — keeping teak warm-toned rather than grey

Natural or synthetic teak?

Natural teak has an unmatched grain but needs regular care. Synthetic teak needs no maintenance, won't soften in heat and offers a wide colour range. We recommend both depending on your use and budget.

Price and timing

Teak decking cost depends on deck area, chosen material and seam condition. Local renewals take a few days; a full deck 1–3 weeks.
