Boat Interior Refit & Upholstery

From upholstery and foam to cabinetry and lighting — we turn your cabin into a more comfortable, brighter space that feels like yours.

Get a Free Quote

Comfort is the key to time aboard

When the interior wears out, time aboard loses its charm. Faded fabrics, collapsed foam, tired cabinetry… At Tekne Usta we take the cabin in hand and renew both looks and function.

Our interior services

  • Upholstery & foam renewal — cushions, upholstery and curtains
  • Cabinetry, galley & joinery — renewing storage and galley solutions
  • Cabin lighting — LED and energy-efficient lighting
  • Interior woodwork & veneer — refinishing and renewing surfaces

Materials made for damp, marine conditions

The fabrics and foams used aboard differ from those at home; we choose marine-grade materials resistant to damp, mould and UV, so the refit lasts.

Price and timing

An interior refit ranges from a few days to a few weeks depending on scope. We confirm at the survey whether you want just upholstery or a full interior, and quote itemised.

What can we renew?

An interior refit is flexible, from a small touch to a full transformation. The table below sums up the main items and their benefits.

ItemMaterial / solutionBenefit
Cushions & upholsteryMarine-grade fabric + quick-dry foamComfort, mould resistance
Curtains & blindsBlackout / shadePrivacy, heat control
LightingLayered LEDEfficient, spacious feel
Cabinetry & galleyMade-to-measure joineryStorage, order

Making a small space feel bigger

Light colours, layered lighting and smart storage make a limited cabin feel far more spacious. For ideas, see our interior refit ideas article.

Solving damp and mould from the start

To make the refit last, we address damp at its source: with proper ventilation and mould prevention, your upholstery lasts. Otherwise even the finest refit soon stains.
